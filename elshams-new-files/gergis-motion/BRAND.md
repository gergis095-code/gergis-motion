# Gergis Motion brand guide

Owner: **Gergis Shawky**. Working name: **Gergis Motion**.
Services: short-form and YouTube video editing, motion graphics, website building.

Use this guide for anything made for Gergis Motion: websites, social posts, thumbnails, video titles, invoices, decks.

## Theme: black and gold (the preferred version)

Luxury, dark and calm. Mostly near-black with gold as the accent and ivory for text. Never bright or colorful.

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#0B0A08` | Main background (warm near-black, not pure black) |
| Background 2 | `#12100C` | Alternate sections |
| Surface | `#1A1712` | Cards, panels |
| Gold | `#D4B47A` | Main accent: highlights, italic words, icons |
| Gold light | `#F1DCAE` | Top of gold gradients |
| Gold deep | `#B8935A` | Bottom of gold gradients |
| Ivory | `#F4EFE6` | Main text on dark |
| Muted | `#A39C8F` | Body text, secondary text |
| Dim | `#8C8476` | Small labels (keeps 5:1 contrast) |
| Gold line | `rgba(212,180,122,.16)` | Thin borders |

**Gold gradient** (buttons, the "Motion" title): `linear-gradient(135deg, #F1DCAE, #D4B47A 50%, #B8935A)`.

Light version, only when a light background is required (documents, invoices): cream `#F4EFE6` background, near-black `#14110C` text, deeper gold `#A07C42`.

## Typography

| Role | Font | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Display / headings | **Cormorant Garamond** (300 to 500, plus italic) | Large, light weight. Key word in *gold italic*, for example "Work that *moves people.*" |
| Body / UI | **Manrope** (400 to 700) | Buttons and labels in uppercase with wide letter spacing |
| Code / data | **JetBrains Mono** | Timecodes, code, technical details |

All three are free on Google Fonts.

## Logo

- **Full logo:** gold "GM" monogram in a thin gold circle with a dashed outer ring, then **GERGIS MOTION** (Cormorant Garamond, uppercase, wide spacing) and **BY GERGIS SHAWKY** (Manrope, small, very wide spacing) underneath.
- **Icon:** the GM circle on its own. Use it for profile pictures, the favicon and video watermarks.
- Files are in `logo/`:
  - `gergis-motion-logo-dark.png`, `gergis-motion-logo-light.png`, `gergis-motion-logo-transparent.png`
  - `gm-icon-dark.png`, `gm-icon-light.png`, `gm-icon-transparent.png`
- Do not stretch, recolor, add effects or shadows, or place the light-text version on a light background.

## Style rules (Gergis's preferences)

- No short dash lines in front of labels or titles.
- Do not invent testimonials, client names, prices or stats. Use real projects only.
- Do not repeat one video everywhere. Feature a project in one or two places at most, never as the background of the first screen.
- He edits short videos and YouTube videos, not films. Avoid the word "film".
- Give website building equal weight to editing and motion.
- Plain, direct wording. Thin gold lines, generous spacing, subtle motion.

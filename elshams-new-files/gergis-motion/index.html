About the studio

Editing, motion and code, all from one person, so nothing gets lost between the cut and the launch.

Gergis ShawkyFounder, Gergis Motion
Booking now

Gergis Motion is my studio. I'm Gergis Shawky, a video editor, motion designer and website builder. I work with brands, creators and businesses that want their content to feel premium, sharp and on purpose.

Most projects end up split across three freelancers: one cuts the video, another animates the logo, a third builds the site. I do all three, so the pacing of your videos, the movement of your brand and the feel of your website come from one consistent creative eye.

My latest project is the complete website for El Shams, a textile dyeing, printing and finishing factory in Egypt: English and Arabic pages, an order tracking system and staff tools, all designed and built by me.

Every cut is timed to the music, every title is animated by hand, and every website is written line by line, with no templates or page builders.

FAQ

      
Good
questions.

      
Don't see yours? Send it with the form below and I'll answer personally.

    

    

      
How do I start a project?
Fill in the project form at the bottom of this page with what you need. I'll reply with questions, ideas and a quote. Once we agree, we start.

      
How much does a project cost?
It depends on length, complexity and turnaround. A 15-second reel and a 60-second ad with motion graphics are very different jobs. Add your budget in the form if you have one in mind, and I'll suggest the best option within it.

      
How long does it take?
Reels and short videos usually take a few days, and YouTube videos depend on their length and footage. Motion graphics and websites take longer depending on scope. You'll get a clear timeline with your quote, and rush delivery is available when needed.

      
How many revisions do I get?
Revision rounds are included in every project and agreed upfront, so you can give feedback at each stage without worrying about surprise costs.

      
Do you build Arabic websites?
Yes. I build Arabic, English and bilingual websites, with proper right-to-left layouts and Arabic typography. The El Shams website is a live example with pages in both languages.

      
Do you handle the domain and hosting?
Yes. I can set up your domain, hosting and contact forms so the website launches fully working. If you already have a domain, I connect the new site to it.

      
Can you build more than a simple website?
Yes. Beyond business sites and landing pages, I build tools like order tracking systems, staff dashboards and forms connected to a database, as I did for El Shams.

      
How do I send my footage?
Google Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer or any link you prefer. Mention it in the project details, or send it after we agree on the project.

      
Do you work with clients worldwide?
Yes. Everything is handled remotely: briefs, previews, feedback and delivery all happen online, wherever you are.

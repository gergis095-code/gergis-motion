# Notes for Claude

This repository holds two separate things:

- The **El Shams** textile factory website (the root pages, `ar/`, `track/`, `suggest/` and the service folders).
- **Gergis Motion**, the personal studio and portfolio of Gergis Shawky, in `gergis-motion/`.

For anything made for Gergis Motion (its website, social content, thumbnails, video titles, documents), follow `gergis-motion/BRAND.md`. In short: the black and gold theme, Cormorant Garamond + Manrope, and the GM monogram logo in `gergis-motion/logo/`. Do not apply the Gergis Motion theme to the El Shams pages, which have their own design.
